Account and Data Deletion

At Köma, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

📋 Data that will be deleted

  • Personal information (name, email, phone)
  • Transaction history
  • Imported contact data
  • App settings and preferences
  • All other data linked to your account

⚠️ Important to know

  • Deletion is final and irreversible
  • Your balance must be zero before deletion
  • Pending transactions must be completed
  • Processing may take up to 30 days
  • You will receive an email confirmation once completed
