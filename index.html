How it Works

Simple as 1, 2, 3

A B2B2C ecosystem where everyone wins

1. Your company subscribes

Create a company account, add your employee beneficiaries and define their monthly benefits

2. Top-up in real-time

Instantly credit your employees' accounts as needed: monthly, ad-hoc or event-based

3. They spend at our partners

Your employees use their benefits at 30+ merchants in Bamako: restaurants, shops, transport...

Features

A complete platform for everyone

Tools designed for companies, employees and merchants

Beneficiary Management

Easily add, remove and manage your employees' accounts from your dashboard

Instant Top-up

Credit individually or in bulk. Balance is immediately available for your employees

Partner Network

30+ partner merchants where your employees can use their benefits: shopping, restaurants, transport

Real-time Notifications

Receive instant alerts for every operation: top-up, spend, validation

Detailed History

View complete operation history with filters by date, employee, merchant and type

Maximum Security

Encrypted transactions, protected data, PIN code validation and enhanced authentication
